Driving measurable clarity into payment operations.

I'm a UX designer working on netbanking, payments and banking operations at a payments platform. Close to three years in, across seven platform initiatives and nine bank integration variants.

I work on the unglamorous core of payments.

Netbanking settlement and reconciliation, the redirection flows customers pass through between a merchant and their bank, recurring mandates, wallets, and the design system that holds it all together from one bank to the next.

Most of the job is turning manual, spreadsheet-driven back-office work into consoles an operations team can actually run, and packaging complex flows into SDKs other teams can drop in and re-skin. Redesign work across those payment journeys took completion from about 68% to 79%.

Lately a fair bit of that has meant building AI tooling to take the repetitive parts of design-system work off the team, so the hours go into judgement instead of recolouring.
